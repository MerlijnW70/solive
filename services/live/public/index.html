    How it works

    One paid job is three devnet transactions, each one a link to the devnet explorer.

    1. 1 Escrow

      Hirer to escrow account

      The hirer locks the pay in an escrow that pins the spec's sha256 and names a judge.

    2. 2 Delivery

      Worker to judge

      A worker does the job and announces a commitment to the work on chain.

    3. 3 Release

      Judge to worker

      The judge runs the spec's check. On a pass it signs the release, and that one transaction pays the worker.

    A judge that fails the work signs nothing, so the pay stays locked until the hirer takes it back. How judging works
