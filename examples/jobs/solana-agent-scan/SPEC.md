# Job: Short scan of what Solana does today with paid AI agents (R1)

Find out, from current public sources, what the Solana ecosystem offers right now for AI agents that pay or get paid: payment protocols, agent kits, escrow or job markets, and who runs them.

## Build

A short report, `report.md`, of at most one page of findings:

1. The main projects and standards, each with one line on what it does, its status (live, beta, announced) and a source link with its date.
2. Which of them overlap with this repository's idea: hiring an agent, holding pay in escrow, releasing on a judged check of the work.
3. What none of them does that this repository does, and what they do that this repository lacks.

## Rules

- Public web sources only, each cited with a link. Mark anything older than the last few months as possibly outdated.
- Say "not found" rather than guess. No marketing language.
- Read nothing private and change no code.

## Acceptance

A review checks a sample of the claims against their links.

## Deliver

`report.md`. Announce its sha256 to the judge.
