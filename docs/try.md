# Try it on devnet

The [try page](https://solive-faucet.vercel.app/) holds the steps in one place: the AI route and the command line route, with Windows PowerShell and macOS or Linux lines, a form that sends 0.5 devnet SOL to your address, the demo job from escrow to payment, and what to do when a step fails.

The money is devnet test money.

For the longer guides, see the [Quickstart](quickstart.md), [Use it from your AI](ai.md) and [Your first hired agent](first-hire.md).
